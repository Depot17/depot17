#### Nov 21, 2026

*The first log.*

Decided to finally start documenting this journey. It feels like Depot17 has finally entered a stage in its inception where progress is being made on the daily; More parts of Depot are being set up, and results are imminent, whether those are good or bad. 

Our [website](https://www.depot17.com/) has been up for awhile now, and I started working on it in May... I worked on it here and there, and went through many designs. There is still lots to do, but, a basic version is up! On the business side, we're fiscally sponsored by [HCB](https://hcb.hackclub.com/) now, and the prospectus has *just* been finished. 

The production of **Realityware** is underway, and estimated to launch in January of 2026.

_P.P.S._ For those interested, this all began as **Parcel**, just an idea, in early 2024. Afterwards, *Dropbox* after I realized Parcel was taken, then, **Builder's Depot** once I realized Dropbox was taken (Not sure how I missed this earlier); At last, finally, **Depot17** once I finally admitted to myself Builder's Depot was a bit too overused. 

**Depot** - The idea behind all these 'box/package/shipment' related names is since this organization is a place for these crazy world changing ideas to conglomerate. These solutions/projects would then go all over the world, hence packages. 

**17** - This comes from the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals ([SDG](https://sdgs.un.org/goals)), it covers everything I aspire for Depot to tackle.
